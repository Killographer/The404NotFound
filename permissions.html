Understand what each permission unlocks and why fake banking apps demand them

Why permissions matter

Banking scams rely on overreaching Android permissions to grab OTPs, scrape contact lists, or hijack your camera/mic. Our analyzer parses the manifest (similar to Sixo’s client-side APK inspection flow [source]) and immediately raises the alarm when a suspicious combination appears.

Use this page to quickly compare high-risk permissions vs. the safe defaults you’d expect from a legitimate banking app.

Scan an App Now
High Risk

Data Exfiltration

  • READ_SMS / SEND_SMS – steal OTPs or spam contacts
  • READ_CONTACTS – harvest victim networks for extortion
  • READ_CALL_LOG – map trusted callers to impersonate

Legit banking apps rarely need direct SMS/contact access in 2025.

Surveillance

Location & Sensors

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION – track precise coordinates
  • CAMERA / RECORD_AUDIO – capture documents or surroundings
  • READ_PHONE_STATE – fingerprint devices and SIMs

Only grant these when the use case is crystal clear (e.g., cheque scan, verified video-KYC).

Baseline

Expected Access

  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE – required for API calls
  • WAKE_LOCK / VIBRATE – keep device awake briefly, haptic feedback
  • READ_PHONE_STATE (limited) – some authenticators still use it

These are normal, but still logged and scored to maintain accountability.

How to audit permissions manually

  1. Run the BroIsThisFake analyzer with the APK or package name.
  2. Review the “🔐 Permissions Check” card in the results for the suspicious list.
  3. Cross-check anything unknown with this page to decide whether it’s justified.
  4. Use the auto-generated Gmail template to report malicious packages to Play Support.
